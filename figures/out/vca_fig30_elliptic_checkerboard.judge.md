m = e^{i pi/3}, M^6 = id (the book's portrait carries no quantitative
claim); pi/3 measurable against a drawn axis; labeled fixed points;

## Named defects (judge) and dispositions

1. **Sparse arrow field** (book: an arrow in nearly every light cell).
   ACCEPTED -- deliberate. The book's dense lens columns read as a
   translation field (design note in the program); the recreation
   documents each region's circulation once and keeps the orbit chain
   uncovered so it stays countable.
2. **Stubby, low-salience small arrows.** PARTIALLY ACCEPTED. Arrows
   are sampled along the true invariant circles (curved, not ticks) but
   deliberately short so no black cell or numeral is covered; salience
   on mid-grey cells is the residual cost.
3. **Faint pi/3 construction.** ACCEPTED this round; top candidate for
   a further pass (same element flagged by the cold reader). The span
   is assertion-checked equal to arg m, so the faintness costs
   salience, not correctness.
4. **Orbit cells 2-4 near-invisible slivers without labels.**
   ACCEPTED -- geometric fact of the bipolar net on the compressed side
   of the orbit band; this is exactly why labels 2-4 sit beside their
   cells (PARAMS comment) and why the near-side band carries no arrows.
5. **Lower tonal separation than the book's white/halftone/black.**
   ACCEPTED -- RISO house theme; the color gate enforces contrast
   floors, and this round's per-label split (paper numerals on the
   black cells) restored the failing pairs.
